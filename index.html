PureVeg Privacy Policy

Effective date: Nov 1, 2026

Contact: purevegapp@gmail.com

What PureVeg does

PureVeg checks packaged food against the diet, restrictions and allergens you choose. You scan a barcode or a photo of an ingredient list.

What stays on your phone

  • Your family member names and each person's settings: diet, health rules, restrictions, allergen alerts and blocked ingredients.
  • Your scan history: product name, verdict and date. For label scans, the ingredient text that was read.
  • Label photos are read on your phone. PureVeg does not save them to your photo library and does not upload them.

You can clear your scan history in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything.

What leaves your phone

  • When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product.
  • If Open Food Facts has no ingredients, the barcode number may also be sent to USDA FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov).
  • Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address. PureVeg sends no name, email, location, device ID or photo.

Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central are separate services with their own privacy policies.

Missing product reports

If a barcode is not found, you can choose Tell PureVeg this product is missing. Your mail app opens a message to purevegapp@gmail.com with the barcode, and the ingredient text if you scanned the label. You review the message and send it yourself. We receive your email address and the text you send. Each week we combine the reports into a list of unique barcodes, without email addresses, to decide which products to add to Open Food Facts.

Feedback email

If you choose Send feedback, your mail app opens a message to purevegapp@gmail.com. We receive the email address and text you send. We use them only to reply and to improve the app.

What PureVeg does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.

Camera

PureVeg asks for camera access to scan barcodes and labels. The camera is used only while you scan.

Children

PureVeg does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Accuracy

Product data comes from public databases and can be wrong or out of date. PureVeg is not medical advice. If you have a food allergy, read the package. Ingredient lists can omit items, and recipes change.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
